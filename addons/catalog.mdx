Every addon in the community catalog, [kubevela/catalog](https://github.com/kubevela/catalog). Each addon's pages are generated from the catalog itself: its parameters, the definitions it adds, its README as a guide, and every version back to the oldest KubeVela release these docs cover.

The catalog opens on the verified addons, from the default registry, grouped by what they do. KubeVela's own addons, such as VelaUX and Vela Workflow, have a tab of their own. Experimental addons are under theirs: they need the experimental registry first, as each one's page shows, and aren't ready for production yet.
